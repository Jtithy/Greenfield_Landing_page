Admission Process

Step 01: Enquiry

Contact the school for admission information.

Step 02: Application

Collect and submit the admission application form.

Step 03: Assessment

Complete the required admission assessment or interview.

Step 04: Confirmation

Complete admission formalities and fee payment.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed Admission Form
  • Student's Birth Registration Certificate
  • Recent Passport-size Photographs
  • Previous School Records (if applicable)
  • Parent/Guardian Information
